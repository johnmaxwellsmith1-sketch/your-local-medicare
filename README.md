# Your Local Medicare

**Free, plain-English Medicare explainers — in under a minute.** Built by a licensed insurance agent for folks who'd rather understand their coverage than be sold to.

## Why this exists

The Medicare / health-coverage gap is enormous and underserved. Most content is either dense explainer papers nobody reads, or hard-sell lead-gen that folks scroll past. This channel sits in the middle: **fast, unbiased, educational shorts** that answer the exact questions people type into Google and YouTube during enrollment season — then route serious viewers to a licensed agent.

## What we cover

- **Turning 65** & the **Mississippi 3-month enrollment rule** — the window you absolutely cannot miss
- **Medicare Part B** premium 2026 — what it costs and who pays more
- The **donut hole** (Medicare Part D coverage gap)
- **Medicare Advantage vs Medigap** — the 30-second difference
- **Extra Help** & dual eligibility
- **Late-enrollment penalties** (Part D)
- **What Medicare doesn't cover** — and the supplement that does
- **Ages 55–64** — how to bridge the coverage gap before Medicare starts
